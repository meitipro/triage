---
title: Tester leaderboard for campaigns
description: Testers ranked by accepted reports and acceptance rate, from the contract, with a CSV for reward or points sheets.
---

Testnet campaigns need a fair way to reward the people who actually tested. The [Testers](/testers) page ranks every
tester by what the validators decided about their reports, and exports the ranking as CSV.

## How testers are ranked

1. More **actionable** reports.
2. A higher **acceptance rate**: actionable reports out of judged ones.
3. Fewer **low-effort** reports.
4. The earlier **first actionable** report.
5. The address, so the order never depends on how the rows were read.

A tester who files a flood of generic reports drops on all three counts that matter: each one is judged low effort,
which lowers the rate and costs the stake. Reports still waiting for the judge count as filed and are shown, but never
as judged.

**Earned** is the rewards of the tester's actionable reports plus any bonuses. Rates are computed in whole basis points
and amounts stay in wei; nothing goes through a floating-point number.

## One program or all of them

The page opens on every program, with the evaluation set (the golden cases) set aside. Pick a program to rank only its
testers; a program page links to its own ranking.

## For a reward sheet

| Request | Returns |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/testers` | JSON, every program but the evaluation set |
| `GET /api/testers?program=2` | JSON, one program |
| `GET /api/testers?program=2&format=csv` | CSV: rank, tester, actionable, judged, acceptance rate, duplicate, out of scope, low effort, waiting, earned and lost in wei, programs, first actionable time |

The ranking is computed from `list_reports` over every program, read from the contract, by `web/lib/leaderboard.mjs`,
which `leaderboard.test.mjs` checks.

## What it is not

The leaderboard is the record, not a verdict on a person. It says what validators decided about each report; it does
not weigh how important a report was. Teams reward importance with bonuses, which the earned column includes.
