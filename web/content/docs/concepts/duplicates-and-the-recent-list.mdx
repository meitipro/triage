title: Duplicates and the recent list
description: How the judge knows what was already accepted, why reports are judged in filing order, and the honest limit.
---

## The recent list

Every prompt carries the program's last 25 accepted reports, newest first, one line each. Two lines from the golden
program, exactly as the contract stored them:

```text
5: Swap button stays disabled after approving USDC, On Base web, the swap button remains disabled after USDC approval until the page is reloaded.
1: Token search misses tickers typed in lowercase, Token search in swap picker fails for lowercase tickers like 'usdc' but works for 'USDC' on Base web/iOS.
```

The id, the tester's title and the one-line summary the leader wrote when the report was accepted. The contract builds
the list from storage, so every validator reads the same lines, and the summaries keep it short and consistent.

## Agreeing on the id

A DUPLICATE names the id it repeats, and validators compare that id as well as the label: two validators who agree it is
a duplicate of different reports have not agreed. The id must be one of the 25 shown. A model that names any other id,
or none, has not given a usable answer; it is retried once and then the round fails, so the report stays waiting rather
than settling on a guess.

## Filing order

Reports in a program are judged in the order they were filed. Without that rule, a farmer could copy a report the
moment it was filed, ask the validators to judge the copy first, and take the reward while the original came back
DUPLICATE. With it, the original is always judged before any copy, and the copy is the one labelled DUPLICATE.

The cost is that a report waits for the ones filed before it. Anyone may ask the validators to judge the next one, so
nothing waits on the team or on a tester who walked away.

## The honest limit

Only 25 accepted reports are shown, to keep the prompt small. A copy of an older report can be judged actionable, and
the tester keeps the reward. That is the honest cost of a short prompt. The team sees the overlap in its own queue, and
the evaluation publishes a partial-overlap case exactly as it came out.

## Before filing

The file-a-report page searches the accepted titles as you type and lists similar reports. It is a hint, not the judge,
and it saves honest testers a wasted report.
