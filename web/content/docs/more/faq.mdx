## What counts as low effort?

A report that is vague or generic ("UI could be better"), has no specifics, or is a list of unconnected suggestions with
no detail. It is the only label that costs the stake, so the rubric defines it precisely. A short report is not low
effort when it is precise.

## Does my language matter?

No. The rubric tells the judge to judge the report, not its writing style or language. A precise bug report in Spanish
is golden case 7; see [Evaluation](/docs/more/evaluation).

## I filed a duplicate by accident. Do I lose my stake?

No. A duplicate gets its stake back and is linked to the report it repeats. Only low effort loses the stake.

## Why is my report waiting for another one?

Reports are judged in the order they were filed, so a copy can never be judged before its original. Anyone can ask the
validators to judge the report ahead of yours; the button on your report's page does it.

## Can the team refuse to pay?

The team never judges. The reward is reserved from the pool when you file, and only validators decide the label.

## What about security bugs?

Never as a report. Reports are public. Use the private contact the scope names.

## Can I appeal a verdict?

There is no appeal on the same report. You can file a clearer report, within the weekly cap and with a new stake.

## Which network?

GenLayer Studio Next, chain 61997. See [Addresses and network](/docs/reference/addresses).

## Changelog

- **October 2026, milestone 1.** The queue reaches the tracker and the record reaches the campaign. A GitHub Action
  opens one issue per actionable report, read straight from the contract
  ([Sync the queue to GitHub Issues](/docs/reference/github-issues-sync)), and Triage runs it on its own repository. A
  [tester leaderboard](/docs/reference/tester-leaderboard) ranks testers by accepted reports and acceptance rate, with a
  CSV for reward sheets. The site also acted on its own queue: CSV export, reports left this week on the program page,
  program names on My reports, waiting counts and trial labels on Programs.
- **October 2026.** First release: one contract with the eleven methods of the build spec, deployed on Studio Next;
  golden cases run through real consensus; the site, these docs, and the queue export.
  Two choices beyond the spec are documented where they apply: reports are judged in filing order
  ([why](/docs/concepts/duplicates-and-the-recent-list#filing-order)), and a duration of 0 days opens a one-hour trial
  program ([terms](/docs/open-a-program#set-the-terms)).
